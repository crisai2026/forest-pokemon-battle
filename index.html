🌲 Forest Pokémon Battle 🌲

Each team has 20 points. Take turns picking Pokémon until you both spend your points — then battle!

🔴 Red: 20 pts left 🔵 Blue: 20 pts left
🔴 Red team, pick a Pokémon

Cheap Pokémon (bugs) let you have MORE of them. Expensive legendaries are strong but you can only afford a few. Both teams get the same 20 points, so it's always fair!

🔴 Red team's turn
Tap one of your Pokémon to start!
Tap your Pokémon → tap a green square to move, or a red Pokémon next to it to attack. Knock out the whole other team to win!
